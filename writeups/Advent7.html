Overview

This challenge focused on using CyberChef to decode and analyze several pieces of data.

Working with CyberChef

CyberChef is extremely powerful, but the sheer number of operations can feel overwhelming. Thankfully, the challenge provided guidance step-by-step.

These were the provided URLs, sanitized in the classic hxxps format:

hxxps[://]cdn[.]bandityeti[.]thm/files/mysterygift[.]exe
hxxps[://]google[.]com/
hxxps[://]www[.]secretSanta[.]THM/Goldenticket/THM_MYSTERY_FLAG
hxxps[://]cdn[.]bandityeti[.]THM/files/index/

Following all steps in CyberChef led to the final flag.
