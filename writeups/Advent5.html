Advent of Cyber — Day 5

Target: TryHackMe — Advent of Cyber (Day 5)

Overview

This task focused on interacting with a VNC service and retrieving the password.

Bruteforcing the VNC Password

I initially wasn't familiar with VNC, so after a bit of research, I realized that I didn't need a username—only a password.

I used Hydra with the RockYou wordlist:

hydra -P /usr/share/wordlists/rockyou.txt vnc://10.10.130.111

Accessing the System

My Kali VM didn't include Remmina by default, so I used the THM AttackBox instead.

After logging in via VNC, the flag was displayed directly on the desktop.
